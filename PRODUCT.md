# ThumbTrek

## Register

product

## Users

People checking their scrolling habits on an Android phone, often briefly between other apps. They want to understand distance, app usage and whether they are within their daily limit.

## Product Purpose

Make scrolling visible through physical distance, history, optional friend comparisons and achievable daily limits. Local tracking works without an account.

## Brand Personality

Clean, playful, friendly. The interface retains the production green identity and a thumb/trail symbol.

## Anti-references

Dense uppercase labels, repeated promotional headings and decorative panels that obscure daily data. The open-bottom distance dial and production green palette are intentional.

## Design Principles

- Lead with today's distance and daily limit.
- Make each screen understandable at a glance.
- Keep social publishing explicitly optional.
- Encourage awareness without shaming people.
- Carry one identity through the app, widget, icon and share card.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support system light/dark themes, large text, labeled touch targets and reduced motion. Use text as well as color to explain state.

# ThumbTrek

## Users

People checking their scrolling habits on an Android phone or in a desktop browser, often briefly between other apps. They want to understand distance, app usage and whether they are within their daily limit.

## Product Purpose

Make scrolling visible through physical distance, history, optional friend comparisons and achievable daily limits. Local tracking works without an account.

## Brand Personality

Clear, energetic, friendly. ThumbTrek is drawn as an athletics track: track blue, white lane lines, a volt-yellow runner and a lap oval, so people read it as a distance tracker at a glance.

## Anti-references

Dense uppercase labels, repeated promotional headings and decorative panels that obscure daily data. The earlier green survey-map identity (contour lines, open-bottom dial, bone paper) is retired.

## Design Principles

- Lead with today's distance and daily limit.
- Make each screen understandable at a glance.
- Keep social publishing explicitly optional.
- Encourage awareness without shaming people.
- Carry one identity through the app, widget, icon, extension, site and share card.
- Tracking is free and needs no account. Premium adds to it and never takes it away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support system light/dark themes, large text, labeled touch targets and reduced motion. Use text as well as color to explain state.

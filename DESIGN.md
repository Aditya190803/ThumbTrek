# ThumbTrek visual system

Retain the production green palette and the open-bottom distance dial.

## Color

Light: bone ground (#F3F1E8), near-white surfaces (#FBFAF4), dark green ink (#171A10), moss actions (#1E6B3E). Dark: green-black ground (#0C0F0B), raised surfaces (#13170F), pale ink (#EDF1E6), mint actions (#8BE6A0). Amber marks streaks; red marks exceeded limits. Chart series use the production categorical palette.

## Typography and layout

Manrope, sentence-case headings, readable captions and tabular distances. Use 24dp page gutters, rounded panels for related controls and four-tab navigation. The dial shows each app’s contribution with the total centered inside it. Daily limit and app breakdown follow below.

## Onboarding

Four resumable steps: privacy, app selection and tracking permission, an explicit limit or baseline mode, optional account and notifications. Baseline mode has no assumed cap or clean-day judgments. Settings allows setup to be revisited.

## Identity and motion

The thumb/trail mark is green throughout the app, launcher, widget and share card. Keep state transitions short and honor the system animation preference.

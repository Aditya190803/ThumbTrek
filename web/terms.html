By using ThumbTrek you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

The software

ThumbTrek is open source and provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Distances are estimates from scroll offsets and screen density. They are good for comparing your own days, not for anything that needs a precise measurement.

Your account

An account is optional and only needed for leaderboards, friends, groups and sharing a limit between devices. You are responsible for what you publish under it.

Leaderboards, friends and groups

  • Choose a name that is not offensive and does not impersonate anyone.
  • Do not publish distances you did not scroll, by editing data, scripting scrolls or writing to the database directly. Rows that are clearly not real can be removed.
  • A group's owner can set its challenge and delete the group. Anyone with a group's code can join it until the group is full, so share codes with people you know.

Delete your ThumbTrek account

Last updated 20 November 2026

You can delete your ThumbTrek account and everything it stored on the server at any time, from the Android app or from the web dashboard. Both do the same thing. It takes a few seconds and cannot be undone.

In the Android app

  1. Open ThumbTrek and go to the Friends tab.
  2. Tap Delete account, next to Sign out.
  3. Confirm. If you last signed in a while ago, Google asks you to sign in once more before the account can be removed.

On the web

  1. Open the dashboard and sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Choose Delete account at the top of the page, next to Sign out.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm. Google may ask you to sign in once more.

If you use ThumbTrek on more than one device, sign out on the others first, so none of them uploads again in the minutes before it notices the account is gone.

What is deleted

  • Groups. Groups you own are deleted for everyone, with every member's numbers in them and the group's join code. From groups you only belong to, your numbers are removed and you leave.
  • Friends. Every friend and friend request, on your list and on theirs.
  • Your friend code, so it no longer finds you.
  • Your private history. Your distance per day, from every device and browser.
  • Your shared limit, if you kept one limit across devices.
  • Your weekly archive rows, the frozen copies of past weeks' leaderboards.
  • Your board row: your name, photo and distances on the leaderboard.
  • Your sign-in: the ThumbTrek account itself, in Firebase Authentication. Your Google account is not affected.

What is kept

  • History on your phone. The app keeps your distances on the device so it works without an account. That stays on the phone until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. The browser extension works the same way until you remove it.
  • The Premium record, only if you ever bought Premium. Whether an account paid, and until when, is kept for billing, tax and legal reasons. Only the billing system can change it; the app and the dashboard cannot delete it.
  • Crash reports the Android app sent before deletion. They are not tied to your account, hold no scroll data, and Firebase Crashlytics deletes them after 90 days.
  • Backup files and exports you saved yourself. ThumbTrek never uploaded them, so it cannot delete them.

If you can no longer sign in

If you lost access to the app or the Google account, email aditya@adityamer.dev from the address of the Google account you used with ThumbTrek, with the subject "Delete my ThumbTrek account". The account and the data listed above are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.
